# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Neo Nomad?

NeoNomad was founded in 2019 out of our need for a better van bed system. After experimenting with custom builds and several iterations, we created the BikerBed. The name 'NeoNomad' reflects our vision of a modern nomadic lifestyle – traveling and riding freely, wherever you want.

## Where is Neo Nomad based? Do you ship internationally?

We are based in Northamptonshire, UK. Currently, we ship standard orders within the UK, but we are happy to discuss international sales if you get in touch.

## How long has Neo Nomad been in business? Are you a small team or a bigger company?

We are a small father-and-son team, designing and building van bed systems since 2019 to solve our own real-world challenges.

## Are Neo Nomad beds made in-house, or do you source them from other companies?

All Neo Nomad beds are designed and produced in-house. This allows us to maintain high quality standards and ensure fast delivery times.

## Can I visit your office or showroom to see the products in person?

As a small operation, we don’t have a showroom or public offices for visits, but we’re always happy to answer questions or provide additional photos and details.

## Can I return a product?

Yes, you can. Please refer to our Returns and Warranty page for full details on the process and conditions.
