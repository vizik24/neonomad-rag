Returns Policy

At Neo Nomad, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you need to return a product, please read the following guidelines carefully.

Eligibility for Returns

  • Returns must be requested within 14 days of receiving your product.
  • Products must be in their original condition, unused, and with all packaging intact.
  • Proof of purchase is required for all returns.

How to Return an Item

To initiate a return, please contact our support team at support@neonomad.co.uk. Include your order number and a brief description of why you are returning the item. We’ll provide instructions for shipping the product back to us.

Refunds

  • Refunds will be issued to the original payment method once the returned product is received and inspected.
  • Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to a fault on our part.
  • We reserve the right to refuse returns that do not meet these criteria.

If you have any questions about returns, please contact our support team at support@neonomad.co.uk.

2-Year Warranty

At Neo Nomad, we stand by the quality of our products. We offer a 2-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship from the date of purchase.

What’s Covered

  • Manufacturing defects or issues with materials under normal use.
  • Repair or replacement of defective parts at Neo Nomad’s discretion.

What’s Not Covered

  • Normal wear and tear (scratches, minor cosmetic marks).
  • Damage from misuse, accidents, modifications, or installation errors.
  • Consumable parts that naturally wear out over time.

How to Claim

If you think your product has a defect, please contact our support team at support@neonomad.co.uk. Provide proof of purchase and photos, and we’ll assess the issue. Eligible items will be repaired or replaced at our discretion. Shipping costs may apply for returns.

Our 2-year warranty is designed to give you peace of mind without hassle.
