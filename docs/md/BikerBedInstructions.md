# BikerBed Installation

## Tools Required

- Electric screwdriver with Allen (hex) bits, or a standard bike multitool

DO NOT use an impact driver.

## Installation Steps

### 1. Position the Base

Place the base roughly on the van floor, with the edge that has no slots facing the rear of the vehicle. Ensure the Hex-Grip finish is facing upwards.

### 2. Install Side Panels and Divider

Insert the side panels and divider, ensuring their orientation matches the diagram provided.

### 3. Install the Front Panel

Insert the front panel and slot the two ribs fully into place.

### 4. Fit the Top Piece(s)

Place the large top piece into position.

- Pre-2026 models: The smaller edge piece can also be slotted into place as shown in the diagram.

- 2026+ models: The separate top pieces are replaced by a single hinged top panel. Once placed, unfold the panel.

### 5. Install the Headboards

Slot the headboards into place, ensuring:

   - The Hex-Grip finish faces outwards

   - The headboard with the larger-diameter holes is positioned at the rear

- 💡 This step is easiest when performed from a standing position at the rear of the vehicle.

### 6. Secure the Brackets

Using a 4 mm Allen key, secure the brackets as shown in the diagram using the 6 provided screws. These should be finger tight.

### 7. Install the Drawer

Move to the rear of the BikerBed.

The drawer slides are pre-installed for your convenience.

1. Move the metal retainer to the front of the slide member attached to the BikerBed.

2. On the drawer, hold the blue lock button and carefully insert the drawer into the slides.

3. Push the drawer closed until it clicks into place.

- ⚠ You should not need to force the drawer. If you encounter resistance, check alignment on both sides and try again.

✔ Verify that the drawer:

- Slides freely

- Locks securely in both the fully closed and fully open positions

### 8. Install the Bike Racks

While still at the rear of the vehicle, select the headboard mounting points you wish to use.

- 💡 We recommend using the highest possible mounting points to prevent pedals from catching on items stored in the drawer.

- Attach the racks using the provided screws (finger-tight only)

- Ensure the rack support piece points downward and is correctly seated in its slot

- Fit the protective covers over the support pieces

### 9. Secure the BikerBed to the Van

Move to the inside or side door of the van.

Using the ratchet strap, secure the BikerBed to the van’s cargo tie-down points:

- Loop the strap over the vertical panels of the BikerBed

- Tighten until secure, but do not over tighten or crush the panels

### 10. Install the Sliding Bed Top

Finally, place the sliding bed top and support into position.

The slots in the top of the middle beam can be used to secure the bed in either the extended or collapsed position.

### 11. Go on an adventure

Load up your bike, and put your BikerBed to good use. Enjoy!
