Thoughtfully designed van systems that let you travel further, ride harder, and sleep better — wherever the road takes you.

Our Story

Neo Nomad began in 2019 with a simple problem — we wanted a better way to sleep in our vans without compromising space for bikes or gear. What started as a simple custom build quickly turned into something much bigger.

After years of prototyping, real-world trips, and countless nights parked at trailheads, the BikerBed evolved through eight generations into the system we build today.

The name Neo Nomad reflects how we live — modern nomads who balance everyday life with the freedom to ride, travel, and explore without compromise.

Who We Are

Neo Nomad is a father-and-son team building products for the life we actually live. We design, test, and refine everything ourselves — and if it doesn’t survive our weekends, it doesn’t ship.

What We Do

We create modular van systems designed around riding, adventure, and comfort — without overcomplicating the setup.

BikerBed

A bed and storage system that securely carries up to two full-suspension bikes.

Kitchen

Compact kitchens built for simple meals at the end of long ride days.

Mattress

Comfortable memory-foam mattresses for proper rest, anywhere.

Accessories

Thoughtful extras that make life on the road easier.

When we’re not building beds

We’re out riding bikes, chasing trails, and living the adventures our products are designed for. Every trip feeds directly back into how we design and build.

Neo Nomad isn’t just what we make — it’s how we choose to live.
