FAQs

Does this kitchen unit fit in every van? How does it mount to the wall?

The kitchen unit is designed to fit in most vans. It mounts securely using a flush-mount system, which also allows for easy removal if needed. Note that if you plan to mount it where a side door is located, that door will no longer be usable.

What exactly comes with the kitchen? Do I need to buy anything else to cook?

The kitchen is a complete standalone unit that includes two removable water tanks (one for fresh water and one for grey water), a battery-powered tap, a sink, and a table. A stove is not included, as customers often prefer to choose their own cooking setup.

How sturdy is the table feature - can I actually use it as a work surface?

Absolutely! The table is very sturdy and can comfortably be used as a work surface for cooking, writing, or other tasks.

Is it compatible with the BikerBed if I want both in my van?

Yes, the kitchen and BikerBed were designed to work seamlessly together. However, the kitchen can also be used independently without the BikerBed.

How easy is it to clean after cooking messy meals?

Cleaning is simple and hassle-free. The kitchen features a durable resin finish that is tough, wipeable, and resistant to stains.

Do I need to connect to an external battery or water tank?

No, the tap runs off of its own battery, and the water tanks are included within the kitchen unit. These tanks are easy to remove, empty and refill using any water source.
