## Are mattresses included?

Mattresses are not included, but you can purchase one alongside your BikerBed. To provide a comfortable mattress at what we believe is a fair price, we actually take a small loss on these, so you’re getting great value.

## How much space does the BikerBed take up in my van? Will I still have room to move around?

In a standard-length Volkswagen Transporter (T5/T6), the BikerBed occupies the space behind the rear seats. When fully extended, it takes up the entire rear of the van, making a spacious and comfortable bed. When collapsed into the sofa configuration, there's plenty of room for all sorts of activities.

## Can I use the BikerBed with the rear seats?

The BikerBed is designed with enough headroom to sit up comfortably or use it as a sofa. To sleep on or fully extend the BikerBed, you will need to remove the rear seats.

## How easy is it to install and remove if I need to switch back to using my van normally?

After the initial setup, the BikerBed can be removed or reinstalled in under 30 minutes. Having an extra pair of hands helps, but it can also be done solo. You can even store it in your van if you need to free up the rear seats.

## Will my bikes get scratched or dirty while stored inside?

No. The BikerBed comes with a custom bike rack lined with thick foam to keep your bikes secure and protected from scratches.

## Can I still use it if I have an e-bike instead of a regular mountain bike?

Yes. The bike rack is designed to hold either two standard bikes or one e-bike. The support bars are fully adjustable to fit different frame designs and sizes.

## Does it work with all types of vans, or are there restrictions?

The BikerBed was designed with the Volkswagen Transporter (SWB T5/T6) in mind. It also fits some LWB vans and other makes/models, including Ford Transits. Please check the specifications section carefully before ordering.

## What happens if I hit a bump while driving—will my bikes rattle or move around?

We’ve been using the BikerBed since 2019 without any issues. When the supports are properly adjusted, the bikes fit snugly and stay secure, even over bumps.
